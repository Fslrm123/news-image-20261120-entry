# THE WORLD TODAY — 20 November 2026

A professional newspaper front page summing up the world news of
**Friday 20 November 2026**, entered in the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

![competition](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261120.svg)
![rank](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261120/Fslrm123/news-image-20261120-entry/rank.svg)
![reward](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261120/Fslrm123/news-image-20261120-entry/reward.svg)

Lead story: Russia's mass missile and drone barrage on Kyiv. Also on the
page: the Trump–Xi–Putin Shenzhen summit, the AI stock sell-off
(S&P 500 −3.7%), COP31 talks overrunning in Antalya, Mexico's capture of
FBI most-wanted cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro", and Peru's 6.8 earthquake.

`main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` (drawn by `draw.py`) to the
output directory. Join make.fun with my referral:
